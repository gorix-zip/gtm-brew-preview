The format

Ninety minutes at the table.

min 90 of 90 minutes

Arrivals

  1. min 0 · 15 min

    Arrivals

    Grab a coffee, find a seat. Nobody’s wearing a lanyard.

  2. min 15 · 10 min

    Introductions

    Name, company, and one experiment from the last month.

  3. min 25 · 40 min

    Anchor share

    One person walks the table through a workflow, a campaign, or a deal they ran.

  4. min 65 · 25 min

    Open discussion

    The room picks the topics. Tables split when the conversation does.

  5. min 90 · wrap

    Takeaways

    Every table leaves with one thing worth stealing.
